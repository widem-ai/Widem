Questions

What does Widem do?

Widem is a Saudi-based software and AI agency. We build custom software, automations and AI agents that take admin work off small and mid-sized businesses, plus the websites, backend and security around them.

How long does it take to build?

Usually 1 to 2 weeks, counted from the day we have your documents and the information we need. We agree the scope on a free 30-minute call first.

How much does custom business software cost?

It depends on what we build. After the free call you get a quote for your exact scope, before any work starts.

Do I have to replace the tools I already use?

No. We can build around the tools you already use and connect them, or replace only the parts that slow you down.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Everything we build works in Arabic and English, with right-to-left layouts done properly, not just translated menus.
