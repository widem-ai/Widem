This website is a presentation page hosted on GitHub Pages (widem-ai.github.io). It has no accounts and no forms, runs no analytics, and sets no cookies of its own.

What this site stores

Your theme (light or dark) and language are saved in your browser's local storage, so the site remembers them next time. This stays on your device and is never sent to Widem or anyone else.

Services the site relies on

  • Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, so Google sees your IP address. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses as part of normal hosting. GitHub's privacy statement applies.
  • Booking a call: the booking buttons open Calendly, a separate scheduling service. If you book, you give your name and email to Calendly, under Calendly's privacy policy. Widem receives these details from Calendly only to hold the call you booked.

Your rights

Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), you have the right to be informed, to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA). For anything Widem holds about you, contact [contact email].

Changes

If the site changes how it handles data, this page is updated and the effective date changes with it.
